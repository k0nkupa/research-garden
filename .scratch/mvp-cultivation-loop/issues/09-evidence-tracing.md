# 09: Evidence tracing signature interaction

**What to build:** The interaction the product is for. A person selects a Claim Leaf or a Harvest and the Tree softens everything unrelated while illuminating the complete path from its Roots through its supporting items to the selection. Disagreement looks different from support. Provenance becomes something they can see rather than something they have to reconstruct, and the panel opens the content at the same moment so structure and substance are legible together.

**Blocked by:** 08

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Selecting an item softens unrelated regions of the Tree and illuminates its complete provenance and evidence path
- [ ] The illuminated path reaches every Root supporting the selection in one traversal
- [ ] Contradicts relationships render as dashed mulberry paths, visually distinct from supporting paths
- [ ] The reading panel opens the selected item's content as the illumination appears
- [ ] Selecting a different item clears the previous illumination
- [ ] Illumination is available to keyboard selection, not only pointer selection
- [ ] The illuminated relationships are also conveyed to assistive technology, not by visual treatment alone
